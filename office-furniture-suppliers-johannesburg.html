Furniture supply for offices, schools and institutions

EDUCORE supports Johannesburg organisations that are furnishing new premises, replacing existing items or procuring furniture for a specific project. Our catalogue includes practical workplace and institutional options, with final product dimensions, finish, colour, availability and delivery arrangements confirmed during quotation.

Furniture procurement often depends on room size, user numbers and operational requirements. Customers are encouraged to provide quantities, preferred dimensions, finish or colour, delivery location and the date by which the furniture is needed. Where a selected model is unavailable, an alternative is proposed clearly and supplied only after approval.

Furniture categories available for quotation

  • Executive and office desks
  • Office and visitor chairs
  • Boardroom tables
  • Reception furniture
  • School desks and chairs
  • Cluster workstations
  • Institutional tables
  • Storage and support units

Planning a bulk furniture requirement

For multi-item projects, send a schedule showing the room or department, product type and quantity. This allows the quotation to distinguish between executive, operational, visitor, training and reception requirements. Delivery access, assembly expectations and site constraints should be communicated before order confirmation.
