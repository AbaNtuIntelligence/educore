Who We Are

A procurement partner, not simply a product seller

Modern organisations require suppliers that understand availability, consistency, value and operational urgency. EDUCORE helps clients source essential products through one dependable procurement channel.

Our supply-focused approach enables schools, government departments, healthcare facilities, corporate offices, NGOs, industrial businesses and other institutions to consolidate purchasing across multiple product categories.
