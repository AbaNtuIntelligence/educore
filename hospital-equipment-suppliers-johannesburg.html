Healthcare equipment sourced against confirmed specifications

EDUCORE supports Johannesburg healthcare-related and institutional buyers with enquiries for hospital equipment, patient-care products and mobility solutions. Because clinical and medical requirements can vary significantly, each request is reviewed against the customer’s intended use, technical specification, quantity and delivery requirements.

The website catalogue demonstrates the types of products that may be sourced. It does not replace technical review or the approved quotation. Product model, dimensions, capacity, included accessories, electrical requirements where applicable, warranty information, availability and lead time must be confirmed before an order is placed.

Equipment categories represented

  • Hospital and examination beds
  • Wheelchairs and walkers
  • Stretchers and spine boards
  • Patient safety rails
  • Monitoring equipment
  • Clinical lighting
  • Crutches and mobility aids
  • Selected clinical consumables

What to include in an enquiry

For an accurate response, provide the equipment name, required model or minimum technical specification, quantity, intended environment, accessories, delivery address and required date. If the request forms part of a tender or procurement schedule, attach the relevant specification pages so compliance can be assessed item by item.
