Cleaning and hygiene supply for Johannesburg facilities

EDUCORE helps organisations maintain cleaner, safer and better-equipped facilities through quotation-based supply of consumables, chemicals, dispensers and cleaning equipment. From our Turffontein location, we serve enquiries across Johannesburg and Gauteng, with nationwide requirements also considered.

Cleaning and hygiene products must suit the surface, facility and intended use. Customers should provide pack size, concentration or specification, quantity and delivery requirements where known. Availability, brand and lead time are confirmed before order approval, and equivalent products are proposed only for customer consideration.

Cleaning and hygiene categories

  • Hand soaps and sanitisers
  • Surface and floor cleaners
  • Paper and tissue products
  • Dispensers and hand dryers
  • Mops, buckets and trolleys
  • Waste and sanitary bins
  • Vacuum and floor equipment
  • Fogging and disinfecting products

Recurring and bulk requirements

Facilities, schools, offices, residential developments, contractors and public organisations may submit a monthly consumption list or once-off requirement. Providing the expected usage, product size and acceptable equivalents enables EDUCORE to prepare a more accurate quotation and highlight any important specification differences.
